# Make room for curiosity

Be kind, specific, and willing to learn. Respect children, adults, disabilities, different experience levels, cultures, and languages. Critique the explanation or implementation, not the person. Do not harass, discriminate, publish private information, spam, or advertise unrelated products.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation. Report concerns to Steven through GitHub without exposing anyone's private information. If a public issue would identify another person, keep the report general and ask for a private reporting route.
