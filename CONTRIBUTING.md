# Help make something worth giving away

Everyday Cool welcomes people with an idea and people who can make an existing explanation clearer. Coding is optional: a better example, caption, translation, or teacher activity can help.

## Propose a subject

Open an idea issue. Describe the curiosity hook, who it is for, what someone can try, and what they should understand afterward. Include reliable sources and a small sketch or example. An issue is a proposal; approval to include it comes from Steven.

## Develop independently

Fork this repository, create a branch, and work in your own copy. For a new workshop, start from `templates/workshop/README.md` and place it in `projects/your-short-name/`. Keep it portable where practical. A simple browser activity is welcome; complexity should earn its place.

You can publish your own fork under its applicable licenses without waiting for inclusion here. Clearly identify your changes and authorship; do not imply endorsement by Steven or the official collection.

## Submit a pull request

Explain the learning outcome, the before/after behavior, sources, licensing, offline behavior, and checks performed. Add a screenshot for a visible change and explain any internet or device requirement. Update `catalog.json` for a proposed new entry, but do not present it as already accepted. Run `python tools/build-packages.py` and `python tools/check-collection.py` if a bundled project changes. Keep unrelated edits out of the proposal.

Steven reviews and decides whether to include changes. We may ask for revision or decline something that does not fit. Do not merge your own proposal into the official repository. There is no automatic approval or publication.

## What fits this collection

- Free access to the actual learning experience. No subscription funnels, ads, affiliate links, paid prompt packs, or mandatory paid AI accounts.
- A concrete activity plus an understandable explanation. Label simulations and distinguish a toy model from a real-world prediction.
- Accurate, attributable sources. Check AI-assisted material yourself and disclose material AI assistance.
- Readable navigation, keyboard access, labels, contrast, and no compulsory motion or autoplay. Include captions or an accessible alternative for essential media.
- Honest offline and internet requirements. Prefer local processing; explain any request sent to an external service.
- Clear licenses for your contribution and its dependencies. Keep publisher media separate and do not relicense somebody else's work.
- Fictional examples. Exclude personal data, credentials, tracking, hidden uploads, and unauthorized copyrighted material.

Keep download helpers understandable, optional, and limited to official or clearly documented sources. No installers, executables, large models, or videos without prior discussion. Do not change existing licenses in a contribution. Contribute original hub code under MIT and original hub educational text under CC BY 4.0; project-specific contributions follow the project's licenses. Identify third-party work separately.

## Corrections and concerns

Use a correction issue for broken links or factual mistakes. For a security concern, provide a general description first, without publishing credentials, personal data, or exploit instructions against real people. Do not put anyone else's private information in an issue.
