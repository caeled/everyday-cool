# Curated by a person, open to contributions

Steven Powell (`@caeled`) is the maintainer and final curator of the official Everyday Cool collection. People can propose improvements through issues and pull requests. A contributor does not gain publishing access by submitting material.

The official collection provides its learning experiences free of charge. It excludes subscription funnels, affiliate promotions, advertising, and paid prompt-pack sales. Curatorial decisions consider usefulness, factual accuracy, clarity, accessibility, licensing, privacy, portability, and the joy of learning.

The existing standalone repositories remain maintained at their current addresses. This repository contains independent copies and a catalog; it does not move the originals or automatically synchronize them. Future entries are developed here or proposed as a source contribution or clearly marked external listing.

Steven reviews changes before inclusion. There is no automatic merging, unreviewed ingestion, or contributor-controlled production publishing. `CODEOWNERS` requests his review; it is documentation and reviewer routing, not an enforced branch protection rule. Repository write access should remain limited to trusted maintainers. If further maintainers are added later, configure a branch rule requiring review before merging.

Inclusion and removal are editorial decisions. Licensing is separate: applicable free licenses permit independent reuse, modification, and commercial uses. Our official free-access policy does not impose an extra noncommercial license restriction on MIT or CC BY work. Independent forks are not automatically endorsed, and their changes should be clearly labeled.

No guaranteed review timeline or support contract is implied. This is a growing educational collection, not an accredited school or certification authority.
