Core Meteorology Equations
F_c = -2 m (Ω × v),   f = 2 Ω sin(φ) Coriolis force deflects moving air right in the Northern Hemisphere (φ > 0) and left in the Southern Hemisphere (φ < 0), vanishing at the Equator.
Ro = U / (f · L) Rossby Number: Ro ≪ 1 means rotation dominates (geostrophic jets & multi-band planets); Ro ≫ 1 means single thermally direct Hadley cells.
