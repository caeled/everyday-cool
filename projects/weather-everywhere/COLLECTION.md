## The Earth Science Wave · 23 November 2026

At Steven Powell's direction, eight earth-science exhibits built by Google Antigravity (Gemini) were integrated into this room byte-for-byte: `earthquake-seismology.html`, `tsunami-shoaling.html`, `atmosphere-coriolis.html`, `plate-tectonics.html`, `glacier-isostasy.html`, `volcano-magma-lab.html`, `climate-radiative-lab.html`, and `river-erosion-delta.html`. No guest file was edited; sha256 hashes of the integrated copies match Steven's uploads exactly. Muse audited each by driving its shipped code headlessly and wrote the eight lessons: `earthquake-seismology-lesson.html`, `tsunami-shoaling-lesson.html`, `atmosphere-coriolis-lesson.html`, `plate-tectonics-lesson.html`, `glacier-isostasy-lesson.html`, `volcano-magma-lab-lesson.html`, `climate-radiative-lab-lesson.html`, and `river-erosion-delta-lesson.html`.

Headline audit results, printed in full in the lessons: Seismology is real PREM ray-tracing (travel times within ~1% of IASP91/TauP, correct shadow zones, S−P triangulation exact) but magnitude is only a volume knob (amplitude ×1.42/unit, no energy computed anywhere) and its shadow zone wrongly shows total P silence where PKIKP still arrives. Tsunami's front speeds track √(g·h) segment by segment and Green's-law shoaling holds from the abyss to ~400 m, then amplitude saturates near 5 m instead of climbing past 10 m, so the page's own Green's-law envelope over-predicts the wave at the beach. Coriolis's parcel cannon is honest and well calibrated (deflection signs right in both hemispheres; magnitude within ~2% of real f) but the Ferrel cell's surface winds blow backwards and the "straight at the equator" parcel curves in its own simulation. Plate Tectonics' machinery is honest (slab geometry, Wadati–Benioff zone, 48/48 magnetic-stripe pairs in mirror symmetry) but its Mid-Atlantic preset spreads at 6.2 cm/yr against the real ≈2.5 and Midway Atoll is labelled 11.5 Myr against ≈28 Myr. Glacier & Isostasy's Airy ratio is exact (917/3300 = 0.278) and its rebound a true exponential on the right timescale, but the advertised "realistic forebulge" does not exist in the shipped math (the flexure kernel is truncated before it can turn negative) and the "900 m crust sink" preset claim fails as seeded. Volcano's viscosity engine gets every direction and both magnitude ends right, but its VEI is a plume-height lookup with no volume in it — effusive Kīlauea grades VEI 3 and the Yellowstone preset never reaches its labelled VEI 7 (and Yellowstone's caldera eruptions are VEI 8 anyway). Climate Radiative's core equations are exact (σT⁴ to ratio 1.00000; the one-layer (1−ε/2) result standard) but its calibration is broken — the "balanced" 14 °C pre-industrial Earth is not a stable state of its own model (it drifts to +32.3 °C), CO₂ forcing measures 7.51 W/m² per doubling against ≈3.7, and its Pinatubo button (−42.5 W/m² at fixed T, ~10× the real event) can tip the planet into a permanent snowball. River Erosion's fluvial geometry passes every direction test (stream-power monotonicity, cut-bank/point-bar, standing-water deposition, base-level response) but sediment is not conserved — the droplet plume mints sediment that becomes real land (5,690 new-land cells in water originally >200 m deep in one preset run) — and its uplift dial delivers 9× its labelled rate.
