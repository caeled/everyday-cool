Physics Notes

In a Buck converter, when MOSFET Q1 closes, current ramps linearly up through inductor L storing ½LI² magnetic energy. When Q1 opens, the collapsing magnetic field reverses V_L and forces the Schottky flyback diode D1 to conduct, freewheeling energy into the smoothing capacitor and load.
