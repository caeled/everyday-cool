Architecture Notes
4-Bit Ripple-Carry ALU: Four cascaded Full Adders compute A[3:0] ± B[3:0]. In Subtract mode, XOR inverters flip B and inject C_in = 1 to form 2's complement (A + ~B + 1). Slow down t_pd to watch the Carry bit ripple gate-by-gate!
