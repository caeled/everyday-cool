Fast m-gates open Na⁺ channels for rapid depolarization (+40 mV); the tethered h-ball plugs the pore shortly after while delayed n-gates open K⁺ channels to repolarize and enforce the refractory period.
Saltatory Conduction & TTX Pharmacology
Wrapping internodes in insulating myelin slashes membrane capacitance and leak, letting depolarizing current leap from Node of Ranvier to Node (~30× faster). Tetrodotoxin (TTX) from pufferfish physically corks the outer Na⁺ selectivity filter, silencing spikes.
Synaptic Cleft & Hebbian STDP
Arriving spikes open presynaptic Ca²⁺ channels, fusing vesicles to release Glutamate (EPSP, depolarizing) or GABA (IPSP, Cl⁻ hyperpolarizing). In circuits, STDP strengthens synapses when pre fires just before post (Δt > 0): cells that fire together, wire together.
