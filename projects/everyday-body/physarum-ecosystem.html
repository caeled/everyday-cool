Biological Note

Physarum polycephalum is a single-celled acellular slime mold with no brain or neurons. Through local chemotaxis, positive feedback along high-flux veins, and photophobic retreat from UV light, it solves Steiner shortest-path transport networks comparable to human railway engineering (Tero et al., Science 2010).
