Pathogen Defense & Epidemic Outbreak — the lesson

The exhibit has two rooms. In the blood vessel, a microscopic immune battle: viruses and bacteria against macrophages, B-cells, killer T-cells, antibodies and memory cells. In the city, a macroscopic SEIR+V epidemic: 320 agents move between four districts and a transit hub, passing through Susceptible → Exposed → Infectious → Recovered, with a fifth compartment, Vaccinated, and sliders for R0, distancing, vaccination, quarantine and hospital capacity. The SEIR skeleton is real and its bookkeeping is exact — nobody is created or lost. But when the audit drove the shipped code and counted what its agents actually do, the city's epidemic turned out to run at a very different intensity from the one its dials advertise. Verdict up top: the population accounting is perfect, the timers are honest — and the R0 slider is nearly inert. The epidemic that actually emerges runs at R0 ≈ 11 whatever the slider says, which breaks the herd-immunity and quarantine presets built on the slider's word.

False claims — flagged prominently

FALSE — the R0 slider does not set R0. The slider (and the “City Outbreak (R0 ≈ 3.2)” preset) promises a reproduction number; the audit measured the real one two independent ways. Counting the exhibit's own secondary tally for the six patient zeros in a fully susceptible city: 55–83 secondary cases, ≈ 9–14 per patient zero (10.5–11.3 per zero in timestep checks at dt = 0.033–0.05 s). Fitting the early exponential growth of E+I gave λ = 0.364 /s, which through the SEIR growth relation implies R0 = 11.0. Worse, the slider barely moves it: at slider 1.5 the patient zeros still averaged 9.9 secondary cases each, and at slider 5.0 only 10.9. The mechanism is visible in the code: transmission is a per-contact-pair hazard (1.55 /s at the default), and agents linger inside the 31 px contact radius for seconds at a time, so almost every prolonged contact transmits regardless of the dial — the emergent R0 is set by how many people an infectious agent stands near during an ~11 s infectious period, not by β.

FALSE — the Herd Immunity preset does not starve transmission. The preset (80% vaccinated, 10% distancing, R0 3.2) promises “Reff < 1.0, starving transmission chains naturally,” and its own Reff display formula does compute ≈ 0.52 at the start. Driven across repeated seeded runs, every single unvaccinated agent was infected — final attack 20.0% of the whole city, which is 100% of the unvaccinated pool — with peak prevalence 13–15% in the preset and 17% at 80% coverage with no distancing. The same full burnout of the susceptible pool happened at 60%, 68.75% (the exact 1 − 1/3.2 threshold) and 75% coverage. That is exactly what an emergent R0 ≈ 11 predicts: its herd threshold is 1 − 1/11 ≈ 91%, not 68.75%. The display formula is not a measurement; the agents outvote it.

FALSE — the Quarantine & Hub Containment preset does not flatten the curve below hospital capacity. The preset (R0 3.4, 45% distancing, 20% vaccinated, 70% quarantine isolation, capacity threshold 20%) promises a curve flattened below capacity. In six seeded runs the peak prevalence was 57–67% — roughly three times the 20% capacity line — and the final attack was again ≈ 80%, the entire unvaccinated pool. Distancing and quarantine as implemented dent this epidemic; they do not flatten it under the line the preset names. (Extreme distancing alone does work — see the credit side — but 45% is not extreme.)

What this is

  • The micro room is a vessel diorama: pathogens drift in laminar flow, macrophages present antigen, B-cells secrete Y-shaped antibodies, killer T-cells induce apoptosis in infected host cells, and vaccination trains persistent memory cells. Its one quantitative promise is the “5× faster” secondary response (measured below).
  • The macro room is an agent-based SEIR+V model — not the compartmental ODEs of the textbook, but 320 individuals with positions, districts, hub commutes, per-agent latent and infectious timers, and pairwise transmission within a contact radius. That is a legitimate way to build an epidemic model; it also means R0 is an emergent property that has to be measured, not a parameter that can be asserted. This audit measured it.
  • The reference model used for comparison is the standard SEIR ODE with the exhibit's own dwell times: latent period 3.3 s (σ = 1/3.3 per s), infectious period 11 s (γ = 1/11 per s), β = R0·γ, integrated independently by RK4. For an agent model a fair band was allowed: peak prevalence within ±10 percentage points and peak time within a factor of 1.5 of the ODE. The exhibit misses that band by a wide margin — and, tellingly, matches the ODE run at its measured R0 far better.

How the audit measured it

The shipped page's script was extracted and run in Node with the DOM and canvas stubbed out and a seeded random generator, and its own stepMacro() was driven at fixed timesteps (dt = 0.1 s for the survey runs; dt = 0.033 and 0.05 s as timestep-sensitivity checks — the results barely moved). Compartment counts, per-agent state dwell times, peak prevalence and the code's own per-infector secondary counter were recorded externally, run by run, across multiple seeds. Nothing in the guest file was edited.

Vaccination mechanics, measured

  • Timing is right. In resetMacro() the vaccinated are assigned state V at setup, before the patient zeros (separate agents) are placed — vaccination really does move S → V before introduction, and the Mass Vaccination button likewise converts only S agents, never E, I or R (measured above: 48 S → V, nobody else touched).
  • Wild-type efficacy is all-or-nothing — and perfect. canBeInfected() returns false for a V agent facing the wild-type strain: a vaccinated agent cannot be infected at all, ever. No efficacy percentage is printed on the page, and now you know not to assume one: the model's vaccine is 100% effective against the wild type, by construction.
  • Against the mutant, the same vaccine turns leaky. The mutation button raises R0 by 0.8 (measured: 3.2 → 4.0 on the slider), directly converts up to 7 S/R agents into mutant infectious cases, and lets the mutant strain reinfect R and V agents at reduced per-contact rates — net susceptibility ×0.525 for R and ×0.25 for V in the transmission code. So the exhibit quietly runs two efficacy models at once: all-or-nothing for the wild type, leaky for the escape variant. The page's text (“partially evades prior immunity”) matches the leaky half; it never mentions the perfect half.

What's true — the credit side

  • The bookkeeping is exact. S+E+I+R+V summed to the population at every step of every run — no leak, no creation, across outbreaks, vaccination waves and mutations. For an agent model with this much movement code, that is a real pass.
  • The dwell timers do what the code says. 1,884 latent spells averaged 3.36 s against a stated 2.2–4.4 s uniform draw; 1,920 infectious spells averaged 11.02 s against 8.5–13.5 s. The SEIR clockwork underneath the city is honest.
  • The unmitigated city really does overwhelm the hospital line — peak prevalence 85–90% against a 22% capacity threshold, as the preset warns.
  • Interventions are not pure theatre at the extremes. 90% distancing alone held the final attack to 2–3% (essentially the patient zeros), and 95% vaccination held total infections to roughly the branching-process expectation for the emergent R0. The levers work; the middle settings are simply far weaker than their labels suggest, because per-contact transmission is saturated.
  • The hub is a real mechanism, measurably. Closing the bridges confined the outbreak to the two seeded districts: final attack 51% — two districts burned completely, two never touched — against 100% with the hub open. “Starving transmission chains” is a thing this model can actually do; it just does it with geography, not with the 80% vaccine preset.
  • At its measured R0, the model agrees with the theory it teaches. Final size ≈ 100% is the final-size equation's answer for R0 ≈ 11, and the ODE at R0 = 11 peaks at the same time as the agents (17 s vs 15–17 s). The mathematics on the page — the final-size relation, the 1 − 1/R0 threshold formula printed in the Vaccinated legend card — is correct mathematics. It is the calibration that is off, by a factor of about 3.4.

Bends named honestly

The Reff readout is a formula, not a measurement. The telemetry computes R0 × (effective susceptible fraction) × (distancing, quarantine and hub factors) and smooths toward it. It never counts a single transmission. That is why it can display Reff ≈ 0.52 over a city in which every unvaccinated resident is about to be infected: it is reporting what the dials should imply, in a model whose contacts don't obey the dials.

Dwell times are uniform, not exponential. Textbook SEIR assumes memoryless (exponential) latent and infectious periods; this exhibit draws each agent's timers uniformly inside hard bounds (nobody is infectious for less than 8.5 s or more than 13.5 s). Uniform timers synchronise the wave — one reason the agent peak (85–90%) towers over even the emergent-R0 ODE peak (49%).

The day clock runs at 1.45× the timers. The counter advances 1.45 days per simulated second while the timers tick in seconds, so on the page's own clock the mean latent period is ≈ 4.9 days and the mean infectious period ≈ 16 days. The page prints no day-lengths for these, so nothing stated is false — but a reader mapping “Day 23 peak” onto a real respiratory virus should know the infectious fortnight-plus baked into that mapping.

The mutant's two sets of books. The Reff formula weights a recovered agent's mutant susceptibility at 0.45; the transmission code actually applies 0.42 × 1.25 = 0.525. (The vaccinated weights do agree: 0.25 in both.) Small, but it is the same disease the Reff readout has everywhere — displayed epidemiology and shipped epidemiology are computed in two places and never reconciled.

Mutation is partly a seeding event. “Pathogen Mutation (Immune Escape)” does raise transmissibility and unlock reinfection — and also silently converts up to 7 susceptible or recovered agents directly into infectious cases. Part of the “secondary wave” it sparks is planted, not transmitted.

Quarantine only ever catches the infectious. Detection checks run on I agents only; exposed agents incubate undetectable and unisolated, and a quarantined agent's contacts are skipped entirely rather than reduced. Real isolation programmes have the same blind spot for the incubating — the bend is that the exhibit's 70% slider reads as a population rate while the code applies it as a per-check probability (×0.55, re-rolled every 1.6 s) on I agents alone.

The micro room's “5×” is assembled, not single. A ×4.8 activation ramp, a ×2.4 firing cooldown and a double antibody burst combine to roughly the advertised speed-up for a memory-trained host. The claim survives as an approximation; it is not one dial set to 5.
