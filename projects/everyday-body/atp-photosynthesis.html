ATP Synthase (F₀–F₁ Nanoturbine)
ADP + Pᵢ + ~3.3 H⁺ → ATP + H₂O
Proton-motive force drives H⁺ through the F₀ membrane c-ring, rotating the asymmetric γ-shaft at hundreds of RPM. Each 120° step cycles the F₁ β-subunits through Open, Loose, and Tight states to forge ATP.

Thylakoid Z-Scheme: 2 H₂O + 8 photons + 2 NADP⁺ → O₂↑ + 2 NADPH + 12 H⁺ pumped into Lumen → ~3 ATP via CF₀–CF₁ synthase.
