What You’re Seeing

Each H₂O molecule is modeled as an explicit rigid 3-site dipole with a 104.5° H–O–H bond angle. Crimson oxygen carries a negative partial charge (δ⁻) while both white hydrogens carry positive charge (δ⁺), creating genuine electrostatic torques and flickering hydrogen bonds.
