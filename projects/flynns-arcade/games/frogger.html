Energy in motion

Nothing in Frogger is powered by the frog. The road lanes and river lanes are two systems running on different clocks, each carrying kinetic energy — ½mv², half the mass times the speed squared. Double a truck's speed and it carries four times the energy. That's why the fast lane feels four times as dangerous: it is.

On the river the trick flips. A log's motion becomes your motion the moment you land on it — energy transferred, not created. Ride a log heading away from an open bay and you're still making progress, as long as you hop off at the right moment.

How to play

  • Road: five lanes, alternating directions, different speeds. Getting hit costs a frog.
  • Median: the purple strip is safe. Catch your breath.
  • River: five lanes of logs and turtles flowing the opposite way. Water is fatal — you must be standing on something. Turtles dive now and then; when they're under, so are you.
  • Home bays: hop into an empty bay at the top. Watch for the bonus fly — worth 200 points.
  • Timer: each frog gets 60 seconds. Run out and the frog is gone.
