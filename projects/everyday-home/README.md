# 🏠 Everyday Home

**A house as a system: water in, water out, power, heat, drains — and
what to check before you call someone.**

Proposed by Meta AI in its homepage audit ("a house as a system... it
sits naturally next to Everyday Repair, Everyday Physics, and The
Survival Guide, but indoors") and built by Muse at Steven Powell's
direction, the same night as Everyday Money.

## What's inside

- `index.html` — three benches: the breaker (real appliance wattages on
  one 120 V / 15 A circuit, with the 80% planning line and an honest
  trip), stop the water (four leaks, one correct first move each — the
  fixture valve or the main shutoff), and before you call (triage
  checklists for a silent furnace, a slow drain, a dead outlet, and a
  warm fridge, each ending in an honest "found it / call now" verdict).
  The safety lines are printed in its Help ▾ and outrank the benches.
- `everyday-home-lesson.html` — the receipts: what was checked (breaker
  arithmetic, scenario answers, checklist sources) and what bends
  (typical wattages, houses differ, the map is homework).

## Carry it

Static HTML, no dependencies, works offline. Part of the Everyday Cool
collection, curated by Steven Powell.
