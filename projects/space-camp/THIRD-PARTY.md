Throw: ideal projectile, flat ground, no drag. Orbit: circular-reference drawing and circular two-body calculations; speed multiplier does not integrate a trajectory. Map: synthetic 51.6° track, rough coastlines, no live orbital data. Visibility ring uses 400 km altitude and 10° geometric elevation, ignoring refraction, terrain, lighting and weather. Mars: Sun-focused half ellipse between circular orbit radii, approximate 259-day transfer and invented initial alignment; not a date-specific prediction or return-mission planner.
