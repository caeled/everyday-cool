Wire the Adder — Gates That Add

The board

Gates sit in columns. A gate may only read the switches, a constant, or the output of a gate in an earlier column — so the wiring always flows left to right, and one pass settles every lamp.

Switches

Flip them and watch the board live. The table on the right grades every combination, not just this one — the row you are standing on is outlined.

Outputs

The lamps the table grades, and the gate that feeds each one.

The contract — the truth table

Every row must agree: your output against the target, for every possible input. Green rows agree; red rows don't — yet. The level is won when the whole table is green.
