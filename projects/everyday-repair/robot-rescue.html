Controls and offline use

Use a mouse, touch, or Tab and Enter/Space. Every control has a text label. There is no timer and no required sound or animation. Open this folder’s HTML files directly in a browser; keep the CSS and JavaScript alongside them. Everything except external reading and searches works offline. Progress and notes last for this tab only; reloading starts fresh.

How the station works

This is a connection puzzle, not an electrical circuit simulation. Tiles have north, east, south, and west ports. Two neighbors connect only when both facing ports are open. The source powers every reachable tile. When the beacon is reachable, Dispatch sends Bolt along a shortest connected route and restores the room. Side branches can light up without reaching the beacon. Hints align one tile on an intended solution; other solutions are allowed.

Hints, undo, and reset

A hint aligns one connection and records one hint rather than counting it as your rotation. Undo restores the previous tiles and counters, including a hinted move. Reset clears only the current mission. A completed board stays locked until reset. There is no timer or score penalty.
