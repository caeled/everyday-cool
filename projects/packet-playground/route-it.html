THE ROUTING GAME

Route It.

A packet needs to cross a small network, and you are every router it meets. No router here knows the whole way — each one knows a single thing: the next hop. Read the table, pick the next hop, and correct at every stop. Head thataway, one hop at a time. That's not a simplification of the Internet. That's the Internet.

Pick a level

Five short deliveries. Each one teaches the table a new way to be wrong — and you a new way to notice.

Score, plainly: delivered or not, hops against par, and traps avoided. Nothing is timed.
