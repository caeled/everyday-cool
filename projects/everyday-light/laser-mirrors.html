Laser & Mirrors

Bounce the beam onto every target at once. Click or tap a mirror to rotate it — each mirror sits at 45°, so it turns the beam exactly 90°: the angle in equals the angle out, every time. That is the whole of reflection, and it is the same law the Light room's benches measure.

One honest simplification: the pale glass blocks in this game let the beam pass straight through. Real glass bends light as it enters — that bending is refraction, and the Light room's lens bench carries the real story. The mirrors here play it straight: reflection is exact.
