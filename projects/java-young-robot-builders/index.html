FROM THE WIDER WORKSHOP

Games that teach — and show their work.

Fifty-three games elsewhere in Everyday Cool keep a back door open: a page that explains the physics inside the game and shows how the game was coded — real formulas, worked examples, and the tricks the builders used. Arranged gentlest first: new readers, start with Laser & Mirrors and Tune the String.
