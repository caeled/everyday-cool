# Everyday Food — Collection Notes

Created 20 November 2026 as an original Everyday Cool workshop, from Steven's idea at the gym: food plants, scout and camping cookbooks, why bitter and sweet taste good together, and the anatomy of the tongue and its relationship to smell. His placement, decided the same day: a main-hub room standing next to The Survival Guide, with links between them — this room's camp kitchen cooks over the Guide's fires, hangs its Dutch oven from the Guide's knots, and obeys the Guide's water rule; the Guide gained return links in its fire section and its Links Out shelf.

The room's headline is the tongue-map audit — the collection's "useful map, not accurate map" theme in one exhibit: Hänig's 1901 measurements, Boring's 1942 graph, and a bench that shades the real, wall-less tongue. Its plant section borrows the neighbour's discipline — a safety line and an honesty line on the cards, and the standing rule in the big callout: never eat a wild plant on a webpage's say-so. The Dutch-oven coal bench reads the published Fears/Chuckwagon chart and prints the Lodge disagreement on its face.
