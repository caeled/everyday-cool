# Sources & limitations

## Bundled material

All lesson/Help text and code are original work written for this workshop (code MIT, lessons CC BY 4.0). The retro radio cabinet is hand-written CSS — no images, fonts, or code from any radio, theme, or asset pack. The banner image is AI-generated art made for this room. No third-party code, fonts, or data are bundled in this folder.

## Played / called at view time (not bundled)

- **SomaFM streams** — the six presets play SomaFM channels from its published public stream addresses (ice1.somafm.com, Icecast). SomaFM is listener-supported, commercial-free radio; the streams are offered for public listening and for third-party players that use its published addresses. Each address was verified live (HTTP 200) on 17 November 2026. © SomaFM. https://somafm.com

## Limitations

The big music services cannot be embedded in any third-party page — they require their own accounts and music licences — so they are linked, never played in-page; only SomaFM plays on the page itself. The dial frequencies are this room's own layout for the presets, not real broadcast frequencies; internet streams have none. Streams are live and shared: a channel can hiccup, change its address, or go quiet, and the page reports a failed stream plainly rather than pretending. Scanner feeds are public broadcasts relayed by volunteers; they can be delayed (sometimes deliberately), can be silent in quiet hours, and are never a substitute for emergency services. WebSDR/KiwiSDR receivers are run by individual volunteers and come and go. Shelf descriptions were written on 17 November 2026; each linked service's own pages are the authority.
