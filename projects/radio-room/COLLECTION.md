# Collection origin

Original Everyday Cool workshop, added 17 November 2026 at Steven Powell's request. In the Everyday Cool ideas side chat that day he proposed "an online radio page for everyday cool? from servies like pandora and iheart to radio.com and brodcastify", decided it should be a NEW room in Everyday Cool, and named it himself: "The Radio Room". He approved the banner concept in his own words ("that sounds perfect") and added the build's key requirement: "If we can a retro interface for the radio" — the in-page player must look like an old radio. Built by Muse (AI assistant) and added directly to the collection at his direction ("Lets give it a try"); this folder is the maintained original, and there is no upstream copy.

The room follows the Games Room shape (a verified shelf of legitimate places, one thing that plays right on the page, a short workshop underneath) and the Find It pattern (Help ▾ menu with full documentation, banner verified on the page and inside the desktop app, reciprocal links — here with Tonight's Sky and A Library, which links back). The updated Everyday Cool desktop app ships as app v1.0.11 through the app repo's existing build workflow.

The playable presets are SomaFM channels played from SomaFM's published public stream addresses (ice1.somafm.com), verified live on 17 November 2026. The big music services (Pandora, iHeart, Spotify, Audacy, etc.) are linked, never embedded — they require their own accounts and licences. Radio.com is noted on the page as Audacy's former name, since Steven listed it as radio.com. Scanner copy states plainly that feeds are public broadcasts and can be delayed. The WebSDR copy's tie to Steven's dad (a ham radio operator since 1956) is Steven's own family fact, stated briefly.

The rules live in `radio.js` (no DOM), checked by `test.cjs`.

Banner: `radio-room-banner.jpg`, AI-generated watercolor to Steven's approved concept — a robot in headphones, hand on the dial of a big old wooden radio, tuning between glowing station markers — generated 17 November 2026 as part of adding this room, in the style and dimensions (2736×912) of the collection's other banners.

Local changes since the room was added (17 November 2026, later that day, at Steven Powell's request — "we can add a broadcastify tuner near it to find local radio"): added the Scanner Tuner, a finder panel under the radio that resolves the visitor's county (browser geolocation or a ZIP/town box, both named by OpenStreetMap's Nominatim) and opens that county's own Broadcastify page — no Broadcastify API is used and no scanner audio plays in the room, because Broadcastify's developer terms offer no embeddable route. Changed files: `index.html` (tuner panel, Help card and menu entry, footer privacy note), `radio.js` (the tuner's rules: all 88 Ohio county → Broadcastify page links, read from Broadcastify's own Ohio page that day, plus the state/front-door fallbacks), `app.js` (tuner wiring: geolocation and Nominatim lookups), `style.css` (the tuner's smaller wooden-box styling), `test.cjs` (county-map and fallback checks), `THIRD-PARTY.md` (Nominatim as a called-at-view-time service; the Broadcastify no-API note), `README.md` (tuner described), and this `COLLECTION.md` (this record).

Local change (18 November 2026, when Everyday Electronics joined the collection): one link added to this room's Keep Going list, pointing to Everyday Electronics — the cross-link Steven asked for when he seeded the Electronics room ("a link to from radio room"), with the reciprocal link in Everyday Electronics pointing back here. Changed file: `index.html` (the one added list item) and this `COLLECTION.md` (this note).
