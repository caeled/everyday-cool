# The Photography Workbench

Everything and anything you wanted to explore about photography — Steven Powell's idea, named in the Everyday Cool ideas chat on 27 November 2026 and built the same morning.

Five live benches on one page:

- **The exposure bench** — aperture, shutter, and ISO traded against a sunny-16 daylight scene (EV 15), with the picture itself answering: brightness by the computed stop error, background blur by aperture, motion smear by shutter, grain by ISO.
- **The lens bench** — focal length 14–200 mm with true angle-of-view readouts and a to-scale sensor shelf (full frame to phone).
- **Depth of field** — the thin-lens equations with hyperfocal distance and a log-scale sharp-zone ruler.
- **Light falls off** — the inverse-square law as a lamp bench: double the distance, quarter the light.
- **The frame** — rule-of-thirds, golden-ratio, diagonal, and centre grids over a scene, with aspect-ratio crops.

Every number is computed from the standard formulas (EV = log2(N²/t); DoF near/far/hyperfocal; FOV arctangents; 1/d²). The banner is AI-generated watercolor in the collection family. No third-party code, no CDN — the page runs whole offline.
