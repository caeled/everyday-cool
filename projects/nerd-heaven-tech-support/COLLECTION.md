Find the Fault and the shelf at ten (21 November 2026): at Steven Powell's direction, this workshop gained its own game — `find-the-fault.html`, **Find the Fault**: four diagnostic cases (a PC that won't POST, "the internet is down", a printer speaking in tongues, a PC that crashes only under load), six faults each, solved by choosing tests and reading the pattern of results, in the method of the room's own aphorism panel — including each fault's odd result, an outcome that looks like it belongs to another branch. The world is an outcome matrix; a greedy solver reading the same matrix proves every case crackable and sets par. `find-the-fault-lesson.html` is its back door (the method, the information math, a case worked in full, the coding choices, the simplifications named — including the printer case's three honest singleton outcomes). `index.html` gained a banner section for the game above the shelf, and the Back Doors shelf itself grew from six lessons to ten, adding Find the Fault, Don't Cook the LED, Wire the Adder, and Shelf It!. The service-worker version in `sw.js` was bumped (`nerd-heaven-v3` to `nerd-heaven-v4`) so installed copies pick up the new home page. `README.md`'s Back Doors section lists all ten, this bench's own game first.
