SOURCES - WHERE THE PARTS OF NET CAME FROM

In Steven's own C: these arrived as download archives in a
networkingTemplate folder - the way a tech actually collected
them. The template ships them extracted and working in NET;
the originals live at:

  mTCP (the whole stack)      brutman.com/mTCP
       mTCP_2026-10-10.zip - current build, from the author.
  Crynwr packet drivers       archive.org, item PKTD11A_ZIP
       PKTD11.ZIP - the era's canonical free driver set.
  Realtek RTL8019 kit         archive.org, item
       rtl8019-devkit-and-drivers - PNPPD.COM is from here.
  Trumpet Winsock 3.0         archive.org, item TWSK30C_ZIP
       Shareware - pointed at, never shipped. See TRUMPET.TXT.
  WINPKT                      archive.org, item WINPKT
       The Windows-layer shim; it ships in NET.

Everything in NET is free software or our own writing.
