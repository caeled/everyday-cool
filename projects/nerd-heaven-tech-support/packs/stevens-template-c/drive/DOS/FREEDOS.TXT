THE FREEDOS BASE IN THIS FOLDER
================================
FreeDOS 1.4, from the project's official package
repository (ibiblio). Free software, mostly GPL v2 -
the licence text is GPL2.TXT here, and the complete
original packages, sources included, ride beside the
template in its sources folder, unmodified.

KERNEL.SYS is the 386 kernel (KERNL386.SYS in its
package), installed under the name a boot sector
looks for. COMMAND.COM is FreeCom, the FreeDOS shell.

STEVEN'S FILES STAY STEVEN'S: his CONFIG.SYS,
AUTOEXEC.BAT and C.BAT were here first and are
untouched. Two of his lines name Microsoft files by
habit; the FreeDOS names, when you boot this for real:
  his root CONFIG.SYS: DEVICE=C:\WINDOWS\HIMEM.SYS
    -> until the Windows slot is filled, the memory
       manager here is C:\DOS\HIMEMX.EXE.
  his AUTOEXEC: C:\Dos\mouse.com
    -> the mouse driver here is CTMOUSE.EXE.
One-line edits, his to make; the template ships his
words as he wrote them and says so.
