THE DOS SLOT

On Steven's machine this folder holds a full MS-DOS: the
system files, EDIT, QBASIC, the works. Microsoft's DOS is
not ours to hand out, so the template ships the folder
with only Steven's own files in it:

  CONFIG.SYS   his CD-ROM configuration - note the banana
               driver and the rem'd-out port/IRQ hunting
               lines. That is a tech's notebook, in place.
  AUTOEXEC.BAT his MSCDEX line for that CD-ROM.
  C.BAT        his directory-spinner: it lists the whole
               drive, forever. Run it when you mean it.

Fill the slot from your own DOS media - MS-DOS, FreeDOS,
whatever your machine honestly runs - and this folder is
the one he built.
