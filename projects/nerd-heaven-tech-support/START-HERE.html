A place to start.

27 practical lessons for learning tech support. No Git knowledge, course account or internet connection needed to begin.

Open the course

First: extract the ZIP

Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All. Open this page from the extracted Nerd-Heaven folder. Keep the files together. You can copy the whole folder onto a USB stick.

Optional: bring the recordings along

On Windows, while connected to the internet, double-click Download-Media.bat in this folder.

Choose four videos (about 3.3 GB), the full fourteen-recording collection (about 3.7 GB), the two-hour starting route (about 195 MB), or transcripts and captions only.

Then open Offline recordings. Saved recordings play without internet. You can run the helper again if a download was interrupted.

On other systems, use the official publisher download links.

Carry your progress

Use Save progress to USB in the course, keep the downloaded progress file, and use Load progress on another computer. Browser notes do not travel with the USB automatically.
