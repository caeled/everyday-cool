01 / The physical world

Redefining Robotics

Google DeepMind · Hannah Fry with Carolina Parada · May 22, 2025 · 44:57 · Audio

What has to happen between “pick that up” and a robot actually doing it? A researcher explains the interaction between perception, planning, movement, and dexterity—and why the ordinary world is such a demanding place for a machine.

Watch or listen at the official source

Internet required. The USB edition includes the saved recording.

Questions worth chasing

  • What does the robot need to sense before it can act?
  • Which parts need fast reactions, and which need a plan?
  • How would you tell whether it learned a useful skill or only one demonstration?

Back in the workshop: physical systems depend on power, connections, devices, and useful diagnostic evidence. Sketch a robot's inputs, decisions, and outputs, then compare that with a PC and its peripherals.

02 / Scientific discovery

AI for science

DeepMind · Hannah Fry with Pushmeet Kohli and researchers · February 16, 2022 · 33:52 · Audio

A tour across scientific problems: genes, fusion, mathematics, and understanding wildlife. The interesting question is how learning systems become useful instruments for discovery—and how scientists know whether their results hold up.

Watch or listen at the official source

Internet required. The USB edition includes the saved recording.

Questions worth chasing

  • What is measured, and what is predicted?
  • What would count as evidence that a prediction was wrong?
  • Which scientific application would you most want to understand next?

Back in the workshop: a support diagnosis is also a testable explanation. Describe one observed fact, one hypothesis, and one test for an imaginary “internet is broken” ticket.

03 / How learning works

Neural Nets

MIT OpenCourseWare · Patrick H. Winston · Recorded Fall 2015 · 50:42 · Video

Here is the mechanism: weights, training examples, errors, and backpropagation. Winston builds the explanation at the board. Some algebra appears; pause whenever you want and follow one example rather than trying to absorb every symbol at once.

Watch or listen at the official source

Internet required. The USB edition includes the saved recording.

Questions worth chasing

  • What is a weight, and how does changing it affect an output?
  • Why do we need an error measure?
  • Why isn't success on training examples enough to show useful learning?

Try a small connection: take the workshop's asset spreadsheet. Imagine predicting whether an item needs inspection from its age, history, and symptoms. Which inputs would be useful? Which might accidentally reveal the answer already? What examples would you hold back for testing?

Spreadsheet practice · Checking AI assistance

Foundational material, recorded in 2015 to update MIT's 6.034 course. It teaches core mechanisms; it is not a tutorial for current AI products. Duration is rounded to seconds.
