# Nerd Heaven — The Tech Support Workshop

A practical introduction to IT support by Steven Powell: the course I wish I had when I was learning by fixing things, mostly on my own.

**27 lessons · 8 subject areas · 4 offline practice benches · No course account or subscription required**

[Open the course](https://caeled.neocities.org/learn/)

## Easy download — no Git needed

[Download the ready-to-use course ZIP](https://github.com/caeled/nerd-heaven-tech-support/releases/latest/download/Nerd-Heaven-Course.zip)

Right-click the ZIP, choose **Extract All**, then open **START-HERE.html** in the extracted Nerd-Heaven folder. You can copy the whole folder to a USB stick. The lessons and practice benches work immediately without internet.

On Windows, **Download-Media.bat** adds optional publisher recordings beside the course. Choose four videos (~3.3 GB), the full collection (~3.7 GB), the two-hour starting route (~195 MB), or reading material only. It needs internet during the download; saved recordings then play offline. No administrator account or extra installation is needed on current Windows 10/11.

## Start learning

Download this repository as a ZIP, extract it, and open `index.html` in a current browser. Keep `app.js`, `course.js` and `style.css` beside the index. You can also clone the repository.

Lessons, explained checkpoints, notes and simulated exercises work without internet. External documentation, recordings and real cloud-service exercises need a connection and, where relevant, an appropriate account. Offline alternatives are included.

## The course

- Troubleshooting, useful questions and clear support tickets.
- Windows, hardware, files, performance and printers.
- Networking: IP addressing, DNS, DHCP, Wi-Fi and evidence gathering.
- Ethernet cabling, T568A/T568B and wire-map faults.
- Microsoft 365 / Office 365 and Google Workspace support concepts.
- Account security, phishing, recovery and responsible support tools.
- A practical portfolio and capstone you can demonstrate.

The starting check helps you choose a route. The network simulator, cable bench, sharing bench and ticket bench let you practice without touching a production system. A printable workbook and mentor guide are included.

## Carry your progress

Browser progress does not automatically travel with a USB stick. Use **Save progress to USB**, retain the downloaded JSON file, then use **Load progress** on another computer. Use fictional data for practice.

## Discovery Room

`discovery.html` links to fourteen substantial recordings from Google DeepMind, MIT OpenCourseWare and ESO: robotics, neural networks, neuroscience, astronomy, protein folding, computing, ecology and cybersecurity. Older recordings are dated so their research context remains clear.

Large recordings are not stored in Git. The optional download helper assembles about eleven hours of media. See [OFFLINE-MEDIA.md](OFFLINE-MEDIA.md).
