The honest box. The live sessions above are real, and they are not ours: fTelnet is a third-party public service, the boards belong to their sysops, and a board may be asleep, full, or gone — the phone book carries the same warning, because it was true of every list ever passed hand to hand. This page's own bench cannot reach the public internet, so the door was audited here for its page and its DOS boot only; the first live ring through it is the visitor's — which is, the bench notes, exactly how it was back then too.
