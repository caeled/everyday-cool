# Collection origin

How to Make a Workshop, added 25 November 2026. Meta AI's homepage audit of the collection proposed three new workshops — Everyday Money, Everyday Home, and this one, "the meta one" — and Steven Powell picked this one up the same night, with the standing instruction that other people be let to contribute ideas if anyone is really watching. Muse built it as a house workshop: the method it teaches is the method the collection is actually made by, and its own build followed the checklist printed on its face (catalog entry 39, hub card, documentation row, sitemap lines, changelog line, provenance map, gate).

**The page** — `index.html`: the five moves (question, bench, audit, wiring, a human decides), the plumbing cards (AI-Delivery, The Baton Box, provenance and the gate), the wiring checklist as a copyable list, the "Bring one" contribution section, and its documentation in an in-page Help ▾, per house rule. **The lesson** — `how-to-make-a-workshop-lesson.html`: this workshop's receipts — the claim (the method as practised, with tonight's hub pass and the sitemap reconciliation as the worked examples), what was checked, and the named bends. `README.md` is the portable guide. `LICENSE` and `LICENSE-CONTENT.md` are the collection-standard license files, byte-identical to the other projects'.

Files: `index.html`, `how-to-make-a-workshop-lesson.html`, `README.md`, `COLLECTION.md`, `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CONTENT.md`.


Forum link (26 November 2026): `index.html` gained the collection's Join our Forum line (Steven's Google Group — anyone may ask to join, members post), the feedback door he asked to have everywhere.
