A photo with strong shapes and good contrast makes the cleanest outline.

Line strength sets the bar for a definite line. Fainter lines still count when they connect to a definite one — that's how a soft outline on a face survives while stray speckles from a busy background drop away. Changing it redraws the outline and starts the colouring over.

← Simple — calmer, fewer lines; best for busy photosDetailed — keeps faint, fine lines →

Simple smooths the picture more before looking for lines, so stone, leaves and fabric stop shouting. Detailed smooths less and lets fainter connected lines through. Changing it also redraws the outline and starts the colouring over.
