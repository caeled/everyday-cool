# Everyday Cool collection copy

Preservation note for the portable collection. This room is an original Everyday Cool workshop, written for the collection in November 2026, and it applies the collection's other science rooms outdoors: the Chemistry room's saponification, the Electronics room's fruit battery, Tonight's Sky's stars, and the Body room's machinery, all working at once around a campfire. Its defining rule — a **safety line and an honesty line on every card** — is the room's own contribution to the house style, written for an audience that includes the Scout troop Steven will share it with.

## What was preserved

- `index.html` — the whole room as one page: fire from little (lays, ferro rod, flint & steel, bow drill, dead-out), the layered water filter with diagram and the big honesty callout, soap & oil (rendering, wood-ash lye, saponification), pantry batteries and the series lesson, direction clues graded A/A−/B/B/D, a ten-card reading-signs section (the world's status reports: a cycling streetlight, clockwork dimming, dark grass, the utility paint code, snow-melt patterns, the gas alarm, the noon streetlight, dry-day water, the wrong hum, an outage's shape), the four-card remedies shelf, the first-aid anchor section with the kit checklist, the links shelf, the take-it-with-you section (a Travel Kit card — this room is one of the app's five rooms — and links to the other four Travel Kit rooms), and the in-page Help docs.
- `app.js` — the kit checklist counter, deliberately memoryless: it counts in the page and forgets on close.
- `style.css` — the room's styles on the collection's shared base, including the amber safety line, the teal honesty line, the big water-rule callout, the filter diagram, and the kit checklist.
- `survival-guide-banner.jpg` — the room's banner (see disclosure below).
- `README.md`, `THIRD-PARTY.md`, `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CONTENT.md`.

## References used (first aid & safety)

The room's first-aid and safety content was checked against, and follows: the **American Red Cross** (life-threatening bleeding steps and the half-a-soda-can measure; tourniquet placement and rules, Nov 2024 article; burns cooled with cool running water, no ice; the 5 back blows / 5 abdominal thrusts choking sequence; CPR/AED class finder), **Scouting America / Scouts BSA** (First Aid merit badge page: kit contents and practice, wound cleaning with clean drinking water, hydrogen peroxide omitted, CPR demonstrated on an approved device), **Smokey Bear / USDA Forest Service** (campfire safety: the lays, the 15-foot rule, drown-stir-drown-feel), and the **CDC** (boiling: rolling boil 1 minute, 3 minutes above 6,500 feet; disinfection limits). Full URLs, what each one backs, and the verification date (2026-11-19, all six links opened and read live) are recorded in `THIRD-PARTY.md`.

## Banner disclosure

The banner is AI-generated artwork made for this room in November 2026: a plush-style white robot wearing a Scout campaign hat (with a small shield emblem) and an orange neckerchief, seated by a stone-ring campfire with teepee-stacked logs, a canvas backpack and a walking stick on the ground beside it, pine forest behind, and a deep-blue dusk sky with scattered stars and the Big Dipper drawn upper-left. It carries **no text or lettering** — nothing to garble — and depicts no real product or person. It ships on its first generation; the robot reads plush rather than metal, which the room happily accepts.

## Preservation boundary

The six outbound links (Red Cross ×3, Scouting America, Smokey Bear, CDC) are pointers to living authorities, not preserved content: they were verified live on 2026-11-19 and are recorded in `THIRD-PARTY.md`, but their pages belong to their publishers and will change on their schedules. The room's own text stands on its own if every one of them moves.
