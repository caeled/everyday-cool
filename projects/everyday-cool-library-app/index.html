The web edition of the Everyday Cool Library App — search five open libraries and read in the page. Wikibooks and Wikisource read natively here; Gutenberg, Internet Archive and Open Library open at their own reader pages, because those sites do not let other pages fetch their book files (a browser rule called CORS). Your own EPUB files open natively too. Places and your library list are kept in this browser.
