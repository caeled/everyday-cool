# Sources and scope

Original converter located in Steven Powell’s Downloads: `roman_numeral_converter.html` (an identical `(1)` copy was also present). Its supplied file is retained as `original-converter.html.txt`; source origin is recorded with SHA-256. No external scripts, fonts, images, or copied teaching resources are bundled.

Further reading, checked 6 October 2026:
- NRICH, University of Cambridge: https://nrich.maths.org/problems/clock-arithmetic and https://nrich.maths.org/articles/introduction-modular-arithmetic . References for extending the clock/remainder discussion; linked, not copied.
- OpenStax, Prealgebra 2e, Ratios and Rate: https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/5-6-ratios-and-rate . Further worked examples of unit rates; linked, not copied.

These publishers retain their own rights. No endorsement or affiliation is implied. Roman input is deliberately limited to common modern canonical forms for 1–3999, not every historical writing convention. Recipes and time displays are simplified math activities with stated limits.
