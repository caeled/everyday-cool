The field is planted after your first click, around it — so the first click is never a mine. That is the modern classic rule, and it is stated here rather than hidden.

Everyday Cool · Numbers in the Wild — Mines is an original Everyday Cool implementation of the classic game, built by Muse; curated by Steven Powell. Every number on the board is an exact census: the count of mines touching that square.
