What this leaves out
  • Colour is escape speed, not the set: The true Mandelbrot set is only the black interior and its infinitely lacy boundary between escaping and trapped points; the jewel glow measures how quickly exterior points flee to ∞.
  • Finite iteration horizon: Points still bounded at Max Iterations are painted black — raising the slider carves out escaping filaments that looked solid.
  • Precision floor (honest math): Plain 64-bit IEEE-754 doubles hit pixelation around 10¹⁴×. Past 10¹²×, Deep Zoom engages a 256-bit BigInt reference orbit + Zhuoran rebasing perturbation, extending depth to ~10⁵⁰× (where 256-bit digits & Float64 delta squares bottom out). The real set continues forever past any zoom we can compute.
