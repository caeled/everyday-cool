This is an abstract 2-chemical Gray–Scott toy model on a flat, static 2D grid — useful for seeing how local reaction + diffusion self-organizes, but far simpler than living tissues:

  • Real morphogenesis couples gene regulatory networks, dozens of signaling morphogens, cell division, chemotaxis, and mechanical folding.
  • No tissue growth or curvature: our grid is uniform, isotropic, and fixed in size, whereas real coral, embryos, and skin expand and curve as patterns form.
  • Deterministic continuum: apart from initial seeding, molecular noise and discrete cell boundaries are omitted.
