Nuclear Cross-Section Notes

Thermalization: Fission emits fast neutrons (~2 MeV, cyan-white). Collisions with moderator nuclei slow them to thermal energy (~0.025 eV, warm gold-mint), boosting U-235 fission cross-section by >10×.

Void Coefficient: Above 320 °C coolant boils into steam voids, lowering moderator density and providing negative self-stabilizing feedback in water-moderated cores.

Breeding & Poisoning: U-238 captures fast/epithermal neutrons to breed fissile Pu-239. Fission chains decay via I-135 into Xe-135, a potent thermal neutron absorber.
