What measured exact

The river model. v_r = −c√(r_s/r), printed correctly: the flow hits c at the horizon and 0.5c at 4 r_s, exactly as Gullstrand–Painlevé says.

Frame-dragging, sign-correct. The audit flipped the spin and the wake flipped with it: at a/M = +0.92 the downstream wake's centroid sat 76.7 px above the axis (asymmetry index −0.483); at −0.92 it sat 78.4 px below (+0.500); the Schwarzschild control sat at −0.037, essentially symmetric. The page promises this experiment, and the page delivers it.

Photon sphere and capture. Printed 1.5 r_s and b_crit = (3√3/2) r_s = 2.598 r_s are exact; the drawn photon circle measured 1.57× the horizon radius, consistent within the dashed stroke's width; telemetry arithmetic matched independent computation in all 12 polled slider combinations; and geodesic tracers confirmed capture at bundle level — of 19 ray crossings upstream, the central bundle inside ~b_crit never emerged downstream.

The detector ring. Chapter 1's quadrupole is real in the graphic: across 36 samples the ring's width and height moved in anti-phase with their sum constant at 341–343 px in every sample, width tracking the signed strain at r = +0.78. The sim's wavelength is internally consistent (crest half-spacing 27–36 px against a predicted λ/2 of 33.5 px), and the ringdown preset's envelope decays 0.380 → 0.001 over 30 s. The wave-optics lens range checks out too: the diffraction parameter is order unity at ~10³ M☉ for 100 Hz, as Chapter 6 claims. House: exactly one network request, zero exceptions, all six presets, views, modes and controls verified.

The chapter that failed — and the fix

Chapter 3 headlines the gravitational Arago spot at λ ~ r_s. Measured in the ⟨h²⟩ view at λ/r_s = 0.95, the downstream axis sits at background (brightness 8–13 against an upstream lit field of 53–247) for hundreds of pixels behind the mass — no spot. An axial caustic does develop at λ/r_s = 1.71 (axis ≈1.5–2× its neighbours at +250 px) and as a broad saturated lobe at 3.24 — an extended caustic in a 2D scalar sim, not the sharp 3D Poisson focus, and the copy carried no 2D caveat. The fix states the measured truth in the chapter and in the live physics box: the axis stays dark at λ ≈ r_s in this bench; the brightening is real at λ ≳ 1.7 r_s; the sharp spot belongs to the 3D geometry, whose diffraction the 2D slice still shows. The same failure shape as LuxLab's Arago preset, one chapter earlier in the alphabet of light — and the same cure: the page now says what its own simulation does.
