What this leaves out −
  • 2D & far smaller: Real murmurations fold in 3D with tens or hundreds of thousands of starlings, not 360 in a flat plane.
  • 7 nearest neighbors: Real starlings watch ~7 closest neighbors regardless of distance — try Starling Mode to switch from a fixed radius to topological 7-nearest tracking.

  • Hawk is pure separation: Birds have no special "predator AI"; the hawk simply enters their separation rule as a nearby obstacle (catch distance = 15 px).
  • Toy physics & sky edges: No real lift, drag, thermals, or roosting instinct; soft viewport margins keep the flock on screen.
