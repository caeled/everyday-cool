What this leaves out
  • 2D equatorial plane: Gravity and orbits run in real SI units (m, s, kg, N) via Velocity Verlet, but in 2D. Latitude honestly scales the planet's cos(lat) spin boost without full 3D inclination planes or J₂ oblateness.
  • Compressed companion orbit: The companion body (Moon / Titan / tide partner) orbits at 9.0 planetary radii (mass ratio μ ≈ 0.022) so both the planet and true 3-body Lagrange points (L1, L2, L4) fit in one navigable view while obeying exact 3-body Newtonian gravity.
  • Single-body mass model: Uses real Tsiolkovsky propellant depletion with multi-stage-equivalent Isp rather than physical stage separation.
  • Simplified aerodynamics: Exponential atmosphere with rotating air and quadratic drag (Fd = ½ρv²CdA); no supersonic Mach wave drag or structural max-Q breakup (extreme heating aborts the craft).
