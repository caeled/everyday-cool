What this leaves out −
  • 2D, not 3D: Every body moves in a single flat orbital plane.
  • Toy G & toy scale: 1 AU = 150 wu, 1 M☉ = 1000 mass units, calibrated so a 1 AU orbit takes 1 simulated year.
  • Gravitational softening (ε = 4 wu): Force uses (r² + ε²)−3/2 so ultra-close passes slingshot cleanly instead of blowing up numerically.
  • Perfectly sticky mergers: Colliding bodies fuse conserving mass, momentum, and volume (r ∝ V1/3), losing kinetic energy to heat/flash — real impacts shatter and scatter debris.
  • No relativity: Pure Newtonian gravity; no perihelion precession or gravitational waves.
  • No tides: Rigid point-mass spheres; no Roche limits, tidal heating, or spin-orbit coupling.
