What this leaves out
  • Non-relativistic: Uses classical F = q(E + v × B) with no Lorentz γ factor (which detunes real cyclotrons at high energy) and no synchrotron radiation losses.
  • No space charge: Particles do not exert Coulomb repulsion on each other.
  • Sharp field edges: Field zones use step-function boundaries rather than smooth magnetic/electric fringe fields.
  • 2D plane: Motion stays strictly planar (no 3D vertical betatron focusing).
  • Electron scale: Electron mass is scaled to 0.25 u (real: 1/1836 u) so electrons and ions remain visible together in one chamber.
