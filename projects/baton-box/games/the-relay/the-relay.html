The Relay — how to play

One Courier Bot. One faraway goal on the right edge of a 10×10 board. Two to four players share the Bot, one turn at a time, on the same device — nobody plays at the same time, and nobody can win alone. You don't have to finish it. You have to set up the next person.

Your turn — 3 Action Points

  • Move (1 AP): tap a neighbour square or use the arrow pad. You cannot walk into walls or open pits, or off the edge of the world.
  • Bridge (1 AP + 1 bridge token): tap “Bridge”, then tap a pit right next to the Bot. You carry 2 bridge tokens. Bridges stay for everyone, for the rest of the game.
  • Scan (1 AP): in Guided it counts walls and open pits within 2 steps and gives the goal distance. In Fog it gives a compass and the distance. In Blind it gives nearby counts only — no compass.
  • Undo: takes back your last action this turn, Action Point and all. Only this turn's actions can be undone.
  • Call for Help: free, any time. The next player starts with an extra bridge token.

Warmer / colder

After each move in Fog or Blind, the game tells you 🔥 Warmer (closer to the goal), 🧊 Colder (farther), or ➖ Same distance — measured in Manhattan steps. In Guided the goal is on the board for everyone to see, so the hint stays put.

End your turn — notes and gifts

  • Leave a note (up to 120 characters) for the next player: “Pit on the right — go north, then east!” Notes are free, and they are the game.
  • Leave a gift if you like: +1 Action Point or +1 bridge for the next player. Gifts and help arrive exactly once, at the start of that player's turn.
  • Then pass the device. The board hides behind a pass screen that shows your note and gift; the next player taps when they are holding it and ready.

Three ways to be lost

  • 👀 Guided — the golden goal is visible. Learn the moves, bridges, and notes. Best first game.
  • 🧭 Fog — the goal is hidden. A compass points the way, moves report warmer/colder, and scans give the distance.
  • 🙈 Blind — no compass, no distance number. Only the warmer/colder feeling after each move — and each other's notes.

Saves and Relay Codes

Saved crews and three game slots live in this browser's local storage — no account, no server. A Relay Code packs a whole game — board, crew, turn, notes, all of it — into one block of text: copy it, send it however you like, and the next player pastes it into Import to continue the exact same game on their device.

The fine print of this web port

  • Meta AI's original saved crews and games through its own saved-data service; that service doesn't exist on a static page, so this port keeps them in local storage instead. The Relay Codes work exactly as before.
  • Every board this port deals is checked before you see it: a path from start to goal must exist that crosses no more pits than your crew's bridge tokens can cover, so a dealt board is winnable by construction, not by luck. In the port audit, Meta AI's unguarded scatter never actually dealt an unwinnable board in 10,000 tries — the field is sparse and the start and goal columns are always clear. The check costs nothing and makes it a promise.
  • The board deals 12 walls and 13 pits — Meta AI's comment in the source says 12 pits, but its code deals 13. The port deals what the code deals, and says so here.
  • This page checks its own rules: open the browser console and run runProofs() to watch it verify board generation, Action Points, gifts, undo, save codes, and a scripted win.
