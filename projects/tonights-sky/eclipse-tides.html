Spring vs. Neap Physics

Gravity pulls harder on Earth's near ocean and weaker on the far ocean, stretching water into two opposite bulges. At New & Full Moon, Solar and Lunar bulges add constructively (Spring Tides). At Quarter Moons, they oppose at 90° (Neap Tides).

Eclipse Optics Notes

Total vs. Annular: The Sun is ~400× wider than the Moon and ~400× farther away. At Perigee (356,500 km), the Moon's umbra reaches Earth (33.5′ > 32.0′ → Totality). At Apogee (406,700 km), the umbra apex stops short (29.4′ < 32.0′ → Ring of Fire).

Blood Moon: During a Total Lunar Eclipse, Earth's atmosphere bends red wavelengths into the umbra via Rayleigh scattering while filtering out blue light.
