# Collection origin

Everyday Money, added 25 November 2026 (catalog 40). Meta AI's homepage audit proposed it as "the missing system — banks, cards, interest, budgets, and scams," tying Decisions Everywhere to Internet Detective to Math; Steven Powell directed the build the same night ("Do Money and Home"), alongside Everyday Home. Muse built it as a house workshop.

**The page** — `index.html`: four benches with the method printed beside each. Bench 1 simulates a card payoff month by month (monthly interest at APR ÷ 12, interest first, then the payment; it names the never-clears case when the payment doesn't beat the first month's interest). Bench 2 runs the same loop with the sign flipped (monthly deposit at month's end, interest credited monthly). Bench 3 is a needs/wants/savings split whose readouts keep the sum honest — under 100 leaves money unassigned, over 100 promises it twice, and the page says which. Bench 4 is four messages judged legit-or-scam with the tell printed after answering (urgency, prize fees in unrecoverable payment forms, secrecy, and one honest message that routes you around itself). Its Help ▾ carries the safety line (pressure + gift cards/wire/crypto = stop and verify on a number you find yourself), the honest limits (real cards differ in detail, not direction; teaching numbers, not financial advice), and the offline/privacy note. **The lesson** — `everyday-money-lesson.html`: the receipts, including the independent recomputation of both loops and the named bends. `README.md` is the portable guide. `LICENSE` and `LICENSE-CONTENT.md` are the collection-standard license files, byte-identical to the other projects'.

Files: `index.html`, `everyday-money-lesson.html`, `README.md`, `COLLECTION.md`, `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-CONTENT.md`.
