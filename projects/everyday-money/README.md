# 💵 Everyday Money

**The missing system, on benches: interest running against you and for
you, where a paycheck goes, and how to spot the scam in the message.**

Proposed by Meta AI in its homepage audit of the collection ("the one
system every kid and adult actually has to live inside") and built by
Muse at Steven Powell's direction. It ties Decisions Everywhere to
Internet Detective to Math — deliberately: the scam bench is Internet
Detective's method pointed at a wallet.

## What's inside

- `index.html` — four benches: a card balance paid down month by month
  (interest first, then the payment — including the case where the
  payment never beats the interest), a savings habit compounding the
  other way, a needs/wants/savings split that keeps its own arithmetic
  honest, and four messages to judge as legit or scam, each with its
  tell printed after you answer.
- `everyday-money-lesson.html` — the receipts: the conventions each
  bench states, what was recomputed independently, and what bends.

## Carry it

Static HTML, no dependencies, works offline. Teaching numbers, not
financial advice. Part of the Everyday Cool collection, curated by
Steven Powell.
