THE GAME · EVERYDAY MEASURE

Anchors.

Nobody feels a number; everybody feels a thing. This game hands you a shelf of anchors — sizes your hands and pockets already know — and asks you to estimate real things against them. Scoring is by ratio: being off by 10 grams is a disaster for a mouse and nothing at all for a car, so the game reads the factor between your estimate and the truth, never the gap. Within ×1.15 is a Whisker. Within ×10 is still the right order of magnitude — and in estimation, that counts. The shelf stays open the whole game. Anchors are tools, not secrets.
