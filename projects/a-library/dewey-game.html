Shelf It!

Every book in a library has an address. Ten rounds: shelve the book where Dewey would put it — or read a number and name what lives there.

Just want the number? Look a subject up.

Type a subject and the table kicks out the Dewey number it lives at — the same hundred divisions the game is played from, searched the way a catalog searches. Where the precise home is a section below the division, you get both numbers: the section is the address, the division is its street.

Searching by subject is a whole trade of its own — the collection's Find It room is built around it. This little lookup is its library cousin.

Choose your rounds

Ten rounds either way. Wrong answers are the teaching kind: the game tells you where the book really goes, and why.
