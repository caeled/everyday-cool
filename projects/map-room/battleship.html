Place your fleet — pick a ship, then click your chart. Ships may touch; they may not overlap.
The search One shot a turn — the classic rule. The shaded squares are the black squares of the chart: every ship afloat covers at least one of them, so they are the only squares a hunt has to check.

Coordinates are read the chart way: letters across, numbers down — C7 is column C, row 7. In the hunt, fire only on the black squares: the smallest ship on either chart is two squares long, so every ship covers at least one black square, and fifty squares of searching do the work of a hundred. Once you draw blood, switch to targeting: probe the neighbours, find the line, and when two hits bracket open water, take the middle square first — halve the interval. The enemy captain hunts the same way, and plays only from what their own shots have shown them. No peeking, on either side.
