# Sources & limitations — The STEM Room

The STEM Room is a landing room: it holds no benches, data, or code of its own beyond the page, its stylesheet, and its banner. Everything it doors into lives in the ten workshops themselves, and each of those carries its own `THIRD-PARTY.md` with its sources, data credits, and limitations — those notices are the authoritative ones for their rooms.

- **Banner:** `stem-room-banner.jpg` is AI-generated artwork made for this collection, in the style of the other room banners. It depicts no real place or person.
- **Third-party code or data in this folder:** none. The page is hand-written HTML and CSS in the collection's house style; it loads no external scripts, fonts, or services.
- **The ten workshops:** Everyday Physics, Everyday Chemistry, Everyday Electronics, Everyday Measure, Everyday Engineering, Everyday Math, Everyday Earth, Everyday Computing, Everyday Body, and The Radio Room — see each room's own `THIRD-PARTY.md` for what it uses and where its material stops being exact.
