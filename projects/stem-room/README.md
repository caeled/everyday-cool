# The STEM Room

An Everyday Cool landing room: the collection's science workshops gathered behind one card on the main hub. The STEM Room holds no benches of its own — it is the shared front door for Everyday Physics, Everyday Sound, Everyday Time, Everyday Light, Everyday Chemistry, Everyday Electronics, Everyday Measure, Everyday Engineering, Everyday Math, Everyday Earth, Everyday Computing, Everyday Body, and The Radio Room, each of which keeps its own page, its own files, and its own web address, unchanged. It opened with six doors on 19 November 2026; Everyday Engineering became the seventh the same evening, Everyday Math the eighth, Everyday Earth the ninth, and Everyday Computing the tenth — the last of the four queued STEM rooms Steven ordered that night. Everyday Sound became the eleventh on 20 November 2026, the first of the wave-2 rooms Steven picked that morning. Everyday Time became the twelfth the same day — the second of the pair in the order Steven set ("start with sound and time"). Everyday Light became the thirteenth the same day — the third of Steven's wave-2 picks, carrying colour theory and its chords the way Sound carries music theory and its.

## What's inside

- **The thirteen doors** — one card per workshop, in the order Physics, Sound, Time, Light, Chemistry, Electronics, Measure, Engineering, Math, Earth, Computing, Body, Radio Room, each carrying the same hook, description, and "start here" line the main hub used to show, and linking to the workshop's existing page.
- **How they connect** — a short section on the ways the thirteen lean on each other: Measure's prefix ladder is spent by Electronics and Chemistry; Electronics builds the crystal radio the Radio Room listens through (the two rooms link both ways); Engineering spends the forces Physics names and walks its six machines into the Survival Guide's field skills; Math is the arithmetic under all of it — its full log table and Engineering's pocket table link both ways, and its Avogadro bench bridges into Chemistry; Earth is the stage the rest stand on — its water cycle, rocks, soil, depths, and ground-read weather, looking down where Tonight's Sky looks up; Computing is the wiring the others imply — Electronics' circuits become gates, Math's numbers become adders, and its toy net proves at toy scale the rule Body's brain explorer states at full scale; Body is the machine the other twelve measure, power, and describe; Sound spends Physics' waves and Math's ratios — its music wing is math geometry with sound — and its hearing cards lean on Body; Time keeps the bench's durations — its sundial is Earth's rotation read as a clock, its pendulum is Physics' own bench grown into a timekeeper, and its shadow walks outdoors into the Survival Guide; Light spends the spectrum the others work by — its colour wheel is Sound's circle of fifths turned to hues, its sky is Earth's air read as a filter, and its shadow bench is Time's sundial geometry walked around in.
- **Help menu** — the header's **Help ▾** menu opens the documentation directly (the thirteen doors, whether the rooms moved, the offline answer), plus a "Full documentation (README)" entry. This README ships inside the desktop app copy, so Help's full documentation works in the app too.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. The page is static — one HTML file, a stylesheet, and the banner — and works fully offline, including inside the desktop app. The thirteen workshops behind the doors work as they always have; only the Radio Room's live streams and scanner feeds need the internet.
