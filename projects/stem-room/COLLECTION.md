# Collection origin

Original Everyday Cool landing room, added 19 November 2026 at Steven Powell's request. In the Everyday Cool ideas side chat he proposed it in his own words: "I want to create a "Stem Room" and move Chemistry, Physics, Electronics, Radio into it with one card on the main" — and when offered the choice, he extended the bench himself: "Build it — Body and Measure go in too." Built by Muse (AI assistant) and added directly to the collection at his direction as the twenty-fourth catalog entry; this folder is the maintained original, and there is no upstream copy.

What "move" means here, stated plainly: the six workshops did not move. Their folders, files, and web addresses are untouched — not one byte of Everyday Physics, Everyday Chemistry, Everyday Electronics, Everyday Measure, Everyday Body, or The Radio Room changed in this commit — so every cross-link between them (Electronics ↔ Radio Room both ways, the Radio Room's links to Tonight's Sky and A Library, Everyday Language's Travel Kit card, and the rest) keeps working. What changed is the hub's front door: the six entries gained a `hubParent` marker in catalog.json, the hub generator (tools/build-hub.py) skips marked entries when it lays out the main hub's cards, and this room's single card stands where the Science block began, immediately after Nerd Heaven. The STEM Room card's catalog description names all six workshops, so the collection checker's rule that every catalog title appears on the hub still holds — and a visitor searching the hub for "physics" or "radio" is still handed this door.

The page itself follows the house shape for a room — banner, hero, numbered sections, Help ▾ menu with full documentation, README — adapted honestly for a landing page: it has no benches and no JavaScript, because its whole job is to hold six doors and say how they connect. Each door's card reuses that workshop's existing hub blurb (hook, description, and starter) verbatim. The updated Everyday Cool desktop app ships as app v1.1.0 through the app repo's existing build workflow.

Banner: `stem-room-banner.jpg`, AI-generated watercolor to Steven's concept — the robot at a long lab bench holding all six trades at once: a glass flask of teal liquid (Chemistry), a green circuit board (Electronics), a plain amber billiard ball (Physics), a brass balance scale with round weights (Measure), a small framed anatomy chart with a plain human silhouette (Body), and a wooden-cabinet radio (The Radio Room) — generated 19 November 2026 as part of adding this room, in the style and dimensions (2736×912) of the collection's other banners. The prompt forbade lettering, and the first generation shipped: there is no text anywhere in the image, the anatomy chart is an unlabeled silhouette, the radio's face is a plain grille with two knobs, and the billiard ball carries no number.
