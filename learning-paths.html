LEARNING PATHS / A SUGGESTED ROUTE

Three ways in.
One way of thinking.

Portable Workshops for Curious Minds

Every workshop stands on its own, and the front page still says start anywhere. These paths simply suggest an order: gentle first steps, then deeper benches, then the workshops that ask the most and give the most back. Every stop teaches how a system works.

Levels are a route, not a gate. Skip around freely — curiosity does not need permission.

PATH 01 / BEGINNER

Look closely, try small things

New, young, or just warming up.
No setup, no accounts, no wrong answers.

Beginner workshops ask you to observe and experiment: compare two places, throw a ball and watch its arc, turn classroom math loose in the wild. The reference shelf belongs at every level, so it starts here — keep it open alongside everything else.

PATH 02 / INTERMEDIATE

Work a bench, solve the case

Ready for real tools.
Reason about what the system is doing.

Intermediate workshops hand you the instruments: codes you can build and break, case files you can investigate, and a complete IT support course with practice benches and tickets. Expect to form a theory, test it, and revise it — the way the real systems work.

PATH 03 / ADVANCED

Take the system apart

For learners who enjoy a challenge.
Real code, real binary, nothing locked.

Advanced workshops ask the most: you will write real Java across nine modules, and take a network apart down to subnet boundaries in binary. Finishing Nerd Heaven first makes Packet Playground’s benches friendlier — and finishing any path makes you the person other people ask how things work.
